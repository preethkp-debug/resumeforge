FAQ

Questions, answered.

An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is the software companies use to filter resumes before a human ever looks at them. ATS-friendly means your resume uses single-column layouts, standard section headings (Experience, Education, Skills), and conventional fonts — so the software can correctly parse your work history and surface it for the recruiter.

Things that break ATS parsing: tables, text boxes, columns, images, headers/footers, fancy fonts, graphics-as-text. Every ResumeForge template avoids all of these.

It's really free. No catch, no "PRO" wall, no watermark on the PDF. The whole tool runs in your browser, so we don't have hosting costs scaling per user, which means we don't need to monetize you. If we ever change that, we'll be loud about it.

Paste a job description into the sidebar on the builder page. We tokenize it, drop common stopwords, and rank the most frequent meaningful terms — typically the skills, tools, and qualifications the role calls for. Then we check which of those terms appear in your resume content and give you a 0–100% match.

Think of it as a free, basic version of what an ATS does on the recruiter's side. Higher score = more likely to surface.
